# Name options

A few directions, since "ADAR Appointment Booking Assistant" is accurate but not very postable:

1. **Adar Front Desk** — clearest positioning. It doesn't replace the practice, it *is* the front desk: the thing that answers, books, and confirms. Works for a clinic, salon, or law office without explanation.
2. **Adar Concierge** — leans into "white-glove," always-available service. Slightly more upscale/hospitality feel than "Front Desk."
3. **TalkBook** (or **Adar TalkBook**) — playful, and the name itself explains the pitch: talk or type, either way it books.
4. **Adar Anywhere** — emphasizes the channel flexibility (call it, text it) over the receptionist metaphor.

My pick for the post below: **Adar Front Desk** — it's the one a stranger scrolling LinkedIn understands in one second. Swap the name throughout if you land on a different favorite; everything else stays the same.
